ENGINEERING PROJECT / 01
ROTATIONAL SYSTEM
01
TEAM LEADER 2025

VRLS — Rotational Satellite Launch System

A team-built engineering prototype exploring a rotational approach to launching a payload vertically.

Arduino Uno Mechanical Design 3D Printing

The idea

I proposed the project concept and led a four-person team to develop a prototype inspired by alternative launch system concepts.

My contribution

I helped develop the original blueprint, handled much of the mechanical and robotic construction, designed the 3D-printable release mechanism and participated in prototype testing.

Testing and recognition

Our prototype achieved a vertical launch height of approximately 10 feet. The project received third place at the HBTU Tech Exhibition in Kanpur in 2025.

This was a student prototype, not an operational satellite-launch vehicle.
